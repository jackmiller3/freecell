How to play

Build all four suits up from Ace to King on the foundations (top right). In the columns, stack cards down in alternating colors. The four cells (top left) hold one card each.

Tap a card to send it to the best spot, or drag it where you want. Tap a card inside an ordered run to move the whole run.

Focus view dims cards buried out of order. Ordered runs stay bright, and so does the next card each foundation needs, so you can see what to dig for.

Hint highlights a move that keeps the game winnable. Tap Hint again to play it.

Rewind takes you back to the last position that could still be won.

Every deal is checked by a solver first. Unwinnable deals are skipped.
